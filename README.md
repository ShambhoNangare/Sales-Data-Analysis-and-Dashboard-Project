# 📊 Customer Sales Insights Dashboard (Excel)

## 🏢 Business Case (Fictional Company)
**InsightCart Retail Pvt. Ltd.** is a fictional retail company name used to
represent a real-world retail business scenario.

The management team faced challenges in tracking overall sales performance,
understanding customer behavior, and analyzing regional contribution due to
the absence of a centralized and interactive reporting system.

They requested a dashboard that could provide quick, data-driven insights
for decision-making.

---

## 🧑‍💼 My Role
I worked as a **Data Analyst**, responsible for:
- Understanding business requirements
- Analyzing real sales data
- Designing an interactive Excel dashboard
- Delivering actionable insights to stakeholders

---

## 📂 Dataset Description
The dataset contains real sales and customer information, including:
- Age Group
- Gender
- Marital Status
- Zone
- State
- Product Category
- Orders
- Revenue

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Track total revenue, orders, and customers
- Analyze order distribution by age group
- Identify top-performing states and zones
- Compare category-wise revenue contribution
- Enable interactive filtering for management

---

## 🛠 Tools & Skills Used
- Microsoft Excel (Pivot Table, Pivot Charts, Slicers, Data validation)
- Data Cleaning and Transformation.
- Sales Performance Analysis.
- Customer Segmentation Analysis.
- Dashboard Design and Storytelling.
- Business Insights and Reporting.

## 📈 Key Insights
- Total revenue crossed ₹10.62M, indicating strong overall sales performance.
- The 26–35 age group placed the highest number of orders, making it the most valuable customer segment.
- West and North zones outperformed other regions in terms of revenue contribution.
- Electronics and Fashion categories contributed the highest share of total revenue.
- Customer purchase behavior shows a peak during mid-age groups, with declining orders in higher age brackets.

---

## 💡 Business Impact
The dashboard helped management to:
- Improve visibility into sales performance
- Identify high-value customer segments
- Focus on top-performing regions and categories
- Reduce manual reporting effort significantly

---
## 📌 Business Recommendation
- Focus marketing and promotional campaigns on the 26–35 age group, as this segment generates the highest number of orders and shows strong purchase behavior.
- Increase inventory availability and targeted regional campaigns in the West and North zones to maximize revenue from high-performing regions.
- Strengthen product positioning and promotions for Electronics and Fashion categories, which contribute the largest share of total revenue.
- Introduce customer retention strategies such as loyalty programs or personalized discounts to encourage repeat purchases.
- Analyze underperforming regions and categories to identify improvement opportunities through pricing optimization, promotions, or better product mix.

---

By analyzing customer demographics, regional performance, and category-wise revenue, the dashboard enables data-driven decision-making and imprive visibilty into overall sales performance.
